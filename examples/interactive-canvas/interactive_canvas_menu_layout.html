Interactive Ink Canvas — Menu layout

Placing the menus with configuration.layout

The canvas UI has 8 slots: top-left, top-center, top-right, middle-left, middle-right, bottom-left, bottom-center, bottom-right. configuration.layout says what goes in each, in stacking order; an occupant left out keeps its default slot. canvas.layout.set(table) changes it at runtime.

Layout
The slot shapes the menu: bars lie flat at the top and bottom and stand up on the sides, and every dropdown opens away from the edge — try the tool menu's shape picker on the side. Two menus in one slot stack: with One bottom bar, undo/redo sits right above the tools.
The Notebook title is a custom occupant, declared in canvasOptions.extend.layout: its factory gets the canvas and its slot (slot, orientation, openTowards), and runs again when it moves.
