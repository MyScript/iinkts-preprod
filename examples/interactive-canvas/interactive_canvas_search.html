Search in Handwriting

Ctrl+F, on handwriting.

The handwritten opening of Pride & Prejudice is loaded and recognized. Type a word or a phrase: every occurrence lights up in the ink, and the list takes you to each one.

canvas.search.find(query) returns the occurrences found in the recognized text and math, handwritten or converted: case and accents ignored, phrases can span a line break, math compares without spaces (3x+2).
canvas.search.highlight(matches, active) marks the matched characters only, and follows the content as it changes.
canvas.search.reveal(match) brings an occurrence into view and selects it.
Try it:
1. Search Bennet, then click Next / Prev (or Enter / Shift+Enter) to jump between occurrences
2. Search a phrase such as Netherfield Park
