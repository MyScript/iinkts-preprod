Interactive Ink Canvas - Live PDF Document

Handwriting → typeset document

Every exported event rebuilds an A4 document from canvas.exportAs("jiix"), entirely in the browser.

Underline a line by hand to make it a heading: the first one is the title, the next ones are section headings. Lines starting with - or 1. become lists.
Equations are typeset with KaTeX from the recognized LaTeX; shapes and arrows are redrawn from the JIIX geometry as numbered figures.
Hover a block of the document to find its ink, or hover the ink to find its block.
The pages follow the canvas theme, and every word, equation and shape keeps the color of its strokes.
Print / PDF prints only the pages — pick "Save as PDF" in the print dialog.
Write and underline a title, add a few lines, a list, an equation and a small diagram, and watch the pages fill up.
